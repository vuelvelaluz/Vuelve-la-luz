Cookie policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser when you visit. They remember information about your visit, help you navigate or, in the case of third-party content, serve measurement and personalisation purposes.

2. What cookies and storage does this site use?

Your analytics choice

We use local storage in your browser to remember whether you accept or decline analytics. This preference is kept for 180 days so we can respect your choice.

Google Analytics, only if you accept

With your consent, we use Google's Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, pages viewed and use of our audio and links. The tag does not load before you accept. You can decline without losing access to any content.

The cookies _ga and _ga_* distinguish browsers and sessions. We have set a duration of 180 days without automatic renewal. We do not enable advertising features or Google Signals.

We do not send names, email addresses or form contents to Analytics. We remove parameters and fragments from URLs sent. Measurement includes technical browser and device information and may include approximate location; it is not strictly anonymous measurement.

You can change your choice through “Cookie settings” at the foot of any page. Declining after accepting stops measurement and deletes the Analytics cookies this site can manage. This does not, by itself, delete data already sent.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and its privacy policy.

Audio and external links

Audio in Practices is served from this website and does not require YouTube or Vimeo cookies. If you open the 21-day app or another external link, that service's terms and preferences apply.

3. How can you manage cookies?

You can allow, block or delete cookies stored on your device through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Blocking certain third-party cookies may prevent embedded videos from playing correctly.

4. Changes to this policy

This cookie policy may be updated as the tools we use on the site change. We recommend reviewing it periodically.

If you have questions, write to asalamamonsalve@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.

Optional oracle workshop measurement

If you allow this measurement, we also count starts and downloads of the free first card using a random browser identifier. We do not save the card, its images, writing or PDF. Reports query only the previous 30 days. Older events are deleted when a new event is received or an administrator views the report; a separate daily task for these events is not promised. Withdrawing measurement requests deletion of the events linked to that choice, as well as purchase attribution.

On the Create your own oracle deck page, the free trial and checkout, you can allow or decline additional measurement, independent of the general analytics for this website. Declining does not prevent you from creating your first card or buying.

The preference is stored in vll-camp-preference for 180 days. Only if you allow it, vll-oraculo-campaign stores the campaign, channel, ad group and, if present, the Google click identifier (gclid, gbraid or wbraid) for 30 days. These first-party cookies are shared between vuelvelaluz.com and taller.vuelvelaluz.com. They do not contain your cards, projects, exercises or downloadable guides.

When checkout begins, the server may associate those consented data with the order and its confirmed payment. We do not count a visit, registration or opening Stripe as a sale. Attribution expires 30 days after the click, and expired attribution records are purged daily; payment accounting records are retained under their own obligations. A collaborator code may accompany purchase links even if you decline this measurement.

Conversions are not currently sent to Google Ads, and advertising tags have not been installed for this workshop. Any future connection will require specific information and the appropriate choice before data is sent. Your private materials will not be sent or used to create audiences.

You can change your choice under “Optional workshop measurement” at the foot of the product, free trial or checkout page. Declining removes attribution from the browser and requests withdrawal of its measurement association on the server; if the connection fails, the notice lets you try again. This does not delete orders, purchases or access rights. Find more information and your rights in our privacy policy.
